A THOUGHTFUL BUSINESS

Keep plant care simple.

The reminder should be free. The extra help can pay for the product.

01

Free plant reminders

Keep the essential job easy to try: organize plants, prioritize watering, and track care. Ads can interrupt a calm routine and are unlikely to earn much before the app has a large audience.

02

Earn on sitter bookings

A later marketplace could charge a transparent service fee on completed bookings. It solves a real problem for travelers, but needs enough trusted owners and sitters in each local area first.

03

Optional paid extras

If people ask for more, test a low-cost plan for shared household access, care history, and custom reminders. Validate demand before building subscriptions or taking payments.
